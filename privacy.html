Shiny Teeth Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Shiny Teeth is an English listening and singing practice app. Its practice audio, text, illustrations, and fonts are included in the installed app. Practice works offline after installation.

Microphone and recordings

The app asks for microphone permission when you choose Record. Listening to the song, Read examples, and word pronunciation does not require microphone access.

Your recorded audio and related information, such as the recording name, time, selected song range, and backing choice, are stored locally in the app on your device. Drafts and recovery information help preserve unfinished recordings. Shiny Teeth does not automatically upload this audio or metadata to a developer server.

You can turn microphone access off in your device’s iOS settings. Recording will then be unavailable until access is allowed again.

Accounts, advertising, and tracking

Shiny Teeth does not require an account or a personal profile, such as a person’s name, email address, age, or school. You can choose your own recording labels; these are stored with the recordings. It contains no advertising or app analytics/tracking SDK. It does not use a live speech-generation service during practice; spoken examples are bundled audio files.

Exporting and sharing

Download prepares a WAV file locally and opens the iOS share sheet. You choose whether to save it to Files or send it to another app or person. An export or share may put a copy outside Shiny Teeth, including in a cloud service you select. Those destinations have their own privacy practices.

Deletion and device backups

Delete removes a saved take from the app’s recording library after you confirm. It does not recall copies you previously exported or shared. Temporary playback/export files, recovery material, or earlier local copies can remain separately in app storage until cleared by the app or iOS.

App data may be included in your device backups according to your iOS and backup settings. The app has no account-based cloud library or automatic recording synchronization service. Use iOS controls to manage app storage and backups, and manage exported copies in their chosen destinations.

Children’s voices and support messages

A recording can contain a child’s voice or other people nearby. A parent or teacher should supervise recording and any decision to export audio or include it in feedback.

If you contact support by email, the recipient receives your email address and whatever information or attachments you choose to send. Support messages are used to respond to your request. Please send only material you intend to share and have permission to provide.

iOS and TestFlight

iOS, TestFlight, and any apps you choose for sharing are separate services with their own privacy controls and notices. This policy describes Shiny Teeth’s app behavior; it does not replace those services’ policies.
